# Mobile UI standards

Targets: native iOS and Android; headless is a behavior harness. Preserve the pure-face first screen (`apps/mobile/app/index.tsx` + `RobotFaceView.tsx`) 1:1: the 620 × 340 cyan/blue circular-eye face occupies the full stage with dual-ellipse atmosphere backlight and bottom horizon glow, while only lightweight peripheral capsules float at the top corners and bottom edge.

All presentation colors live in `theme.json`. Cyan (`primary` / `eye-highlight` / `eye`) belongs to the face, active presence dot and subtitle ornament; electric blue (`brand-blue` / `live-text`) belongs to the `LIVE 对话` pill and wake-guide highlights; green (`verified`) belongs to active LIVE listening/speaking states; coral (`destructive` / `agent-stop`) belongs to stop/failure. Do not synthesize audio amplitudes; waveform bars are driven by real microphone capture and TTS playback RMS.

The first screen (`App.tsx`) keeps a strict 3-zone peripheral hierarchy:
- **Top-left (`ambientPresence`)**: Semi-transparent status dot + 11pt status caption (`陪伴待机` / `实时聆听中` / `Agent 思考中` / `LOOI 说话中`).
- **Top-right (`rightControls`)**: Compact 34pt glass pills (`LIVE 对话`, `对话` toggling the inline floating `askBar` below the top row, and `连接` opening the unified connection settings dialog).
- **Bottom (`WakeGuideBar` / `ResponsePreview`)**: Symmetric 7-bar acoustic wave ornaments + acoustic parenthesis `( 说 Hey LOOI 唤醒我 · 轻触开启 LIVE 对话 )` when idle before dismissal, replaced by the glass subtitle capsule when a transcript or reply is active (tappable to open the full `LOOI 的回复` dialog).

Secondary panels (`HomePanel`, `TasksPanel`, `VoicePanel`, and `新对话`) are accessed from inside `连接设置` rather than cluttering the first screen. Approval always identifies the operation and its parameters; file write approval includes the exact proposed content. Allow-once references are generated by the current UI press and bound to that session.

One native Canvas (`ui/RobotFace.tsx`) draws the 39 × 21 dot texture, bottom horizon glow, dual-ellipse atmosphere glow, optical 3D eye orbs (radial gradient shifted upward so top/sides stay bright cyan and only the lower crescent transitions to `#0E84FF` / `#0051FF` with an inset 3D bottom rim, top-left specular highlight and curious/listening sparkle), conditional eyebrow arcs (never drawn in `neutral` or `speaking`), and the mouth/waveform in the centered 620 × 340 coordinate system. Scale eye radius uniformly; never squash circles into ellipses.
